Executive Summary

Pushing Academic Boundaries, Together

The strategic partnership between Nagarjuna College of Engineering and Technology and Technical Hub has always been driven by a single ambition — to push academic boundaries and prepare students for the world they will actually graduate into. Its most recent chapter opens an entirely new frontier: artificial intelligence, applied not as a buzzword, but as a working discipline across the campus.

Rather than treating AI as an isolated technical skill confined to a single course, Technical Hub has seamlessly embedded Claude AI into NCET's everyday operational and academic cycles. The result is a foundational shift — from occasional experimentation to an institution-wide culture where intelligent assistance is simply part of how teaching, learning, and building get done.

Cultivating an AI Culture · 3-Day Program

Claude AI Awareness Program @ NTSquare

A three-day Claude AI Awareness Program was conducted at NTSquare, NCET, introducing students to the practical applications of modern artificial intelligence. Centred on a live campus kiosk, the program demystified Claude AI and its role across learning, coding, content creation, research, problem-solving, and everyday productivity.

“Meet Claude — Your AI Thinking Partner”: a live, on-campus kiosk drawing students in across all three days

Live Demonstrations & Real Use Cases

Through interactive discussions and real-time demonstrations, students watched Claude debug code, unpack complex engineering concepts, outline projects from scratch, and compress hours of routine work into minutes — turning first-time curiosity into a daily working habit.

Effective Prompting & Responsible AI

Beyond the “wow” moment, the sessions grounded students in the fundamentals that matter: writing effective prompts, using AI responsibly, and applying Claude to genuine academic and professional growth — a valuable step toward AI awareness and digital readiness at NCET.

Claude Training & Certification

The Claude Training and Certification program at NCET introduces students and professionals to the real power of Claude AI and modern, AI-assisted productivity. Through practical, hands-on learning, participants gain the confidence to apply Claude across academic, technical, and professional tasks — and earn a credential that proves it.
